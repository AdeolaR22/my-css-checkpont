GOOD TO KNOW

A Few Things You Might Be Wondering.

What does a private chef service include?

Depending on your booking, the service may include menu planning, ingredient sourcing, preparation, cooking, plating, and agreed kitchen cleanup.

Can I customize my menu?

Absolutely. Every menu can be adapted to your preferences, dietary requirements, and occasion.

How far in advance should I book?

Booking in advance is recommended, especially for weekends and special occasions.

Do you cater for large events?

Event size depends on the menu, location, staffing, and kitchen facilities. Share your guest count so we can discuss your requirements.
